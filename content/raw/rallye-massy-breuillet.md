# Rallye Massy-Breuillet — https://www.vcmp91.fr/rallye-massy-breuillet

**Date:** Sunday, 1 November 2026

**Description:** "Organisé tous les ans lors du premier week-end de novembre, le rallye du club clôture la saison cyclotouriste en Essonne." (Held annually the first weekend of November, the club's flagship rally closes the cyclotourism season in Essonne.)

## Three routes
1. **91 km** (813 m elevation) — rest stops Breuillet (km 30) and Marcoussis (km 75). Route: openrunner.com/route-details/15713203
2. **62 km** (670 m elevation) — rest stops Breuillet (km 30, moved to town hall entrance near the railway crossing) and Marcoussis (km 47). Route: openrunner.com/route-details/15713109
3. **35 km** (350 m elevation) — one rest stop, Marcoussis (km 20). Route: openrunner.com/route-details/15713040
