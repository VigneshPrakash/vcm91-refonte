## Main Text Content

**Header:** "Vélo Club Massy 91"

**Welcome Message:** "Bienvenue sur le site du Vélo Club Massy 91, club de cyclotourisme créé en 1948, affilié à la Fédération Française de Cyclotourisme"

**Alert Notice:** Club will participate in the City of Massy's Association Forum on September 5, 2026, 10 AM-6 PM at Georges-Brassens Park, stand B-105.

**Club Activities:**
- Sunday morning group rides
- Weekend randonnées and rallies with neighboring clubs
- Trial membership option (3 rides without commitment)
- Annual club retreat
- Family outings and recreational events
- Participation in Massy Children's Sports Festival

**General Information:**
- Meeting location for Sunday rides: Recyclerie Sportive de Massy parking lot
- Club office located at Recyclerie Sportive de Massy, Place Pierre Semard (former RER C station)
- Program details available in "Programmes des groupes" section
- Latest updates in "La vie du Club" section

**Contact:** vcmp91@gmail.com
