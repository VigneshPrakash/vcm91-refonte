# Histoire du Club — https://www.vcmp91.fr/histoire-du-club

## Timeline
- **1948** — Club relaunched as Vélo Club Massy Palaiseau by Paul Bouvy (ex-pro rider) and Pierre Marie.
- **1938, 1942, 1945, 1950-1952** — Henri Lemoine's championship wins (source of the club's polka-dot detail).
- **1968** — Hosted the finish of the Bordeaux-Paris professional race.
- **1977** — Cyclotourisme section established.
- **1980** — Cycling school created, ages 10+.
- **1982** — Cyclosport section launched (later discontinued).
- **1986** — BMX section created (still operating today).
- **Early 2000s** — Massy-Coulomb long-distance ride discontinued.
- **Early 2010s** — Cycling school closed.

## Narrative
"En 1948, sous l'impulsion de Paul Bouvy (ancien coureur pro) et Pierre Marie, le club est relancé sous le nom du Vélo Club Massy Palaiseau." The club's kit is blue with a white stripe, with Henri Lemoine's three polka dots added after his national championship wins.

Programme historically included two annual cyclotourisme events — Massy-Coulomb (up to 180 km) and Massy-Breuillet (up to 90 km) — plus family outings, May weekend rides, and social gatherings.
